# Feature Request: Interactive Product Demonstration

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Requestor:** Founder
**Type:** New Feature - Marketing Critical

---

## Raw Request

Replace the current "See a MemoryPop" CTA with a true interactive product demonstration.

---

## Problem Statement

The current CTA "See a MemoryPop" is misleading.

Users expect to experience the product. Instead they are taken into the creation flow.

This creates unnecessary friction because many first-time visitors still don't understand what a MemoryPop actually is.

People should understand the product before we ask them to create one.

---

## Product Goal

Replace "See a MemoryPop" with a genuine interactive demonstration.

This should answer:
- What is a MemoryPop?
- What does it feel like?
- What does the recipient experience?
- Why would I create one?
- Why would I choose Premium?

---

## Product Principle

Do NOT build a marketing page.

Build a believable celebration.

Visitors should feel like they have genuinely been invited into somebody's MemoryPop.

Use realistic contributors.
Use authentic messages.
Use believable photos.

Create enough content that people naturally continue scrolling because they want to discover what happens next.

The goal is emotional engagement.

People should finish the experience thinking: "I want to create something like this."

---

## Suggested Demo Story

Use a realistic example such as: Emma's 30th Birthday

The visitor experiences:
• Welcome screen
↓
• Celebration cover
↓
• Personal messages
↓
• Photos
↓
• Funny memories
↓
• Heartfelt memories
↓
• Recipient reactions
↓
• Creator dashboard
↓
• "Create your own MemoryPop"

The story should feel authentic and emotionally engaging.

---

## Standard vs Premium

Do NOT simply show a pricing table.

Instead let visitors experience both.

Near the end of the demo introduce: Standard vs Premium

Allow visitors to switch between the two.

When Premium is selected, demonstrate the richer experience visually.

Examples:

**Standard:**
- Beautiful celebration
- Messages
- Photos
- Reactions

**Premium:**
- Enhanced presentation
- Richer reveal
- Premium styling
- Keepsake experience
- Future premium capabilities

The visitor should immediately understand why Premium feels more special.

Show. Don't tell.

---

## Strategic Importance

This is not simply another page.

This should become the canonical MemoryPop demonstration.

Over time it should become the destination used for:
- Homepage CTA
- SEO landing pages
- Product launches
- PR articles
- Investor presentations
- Social media campaigns
- Email marketing
- Paid advertising
- Customer onboarding

Whenever somebody asks: "What is MemoryPop?"
The answer should be: "Experience one."

Design this so it can become one of MemoryPop's strongest marketing assets.

---

## UX Principles

- Mobile-first
- Fast loading
- Beautiful
- Emotionally engaging
- Interactive
- No login required
- SEO-friendly
- Accessible
- Warm and human

Avoid corporate marketing language.

The product should sell itself through the experience.

---

## Future Extensibility

Design the architecture so this demo can later support:
- Different occasions
- Different moods
- Premium demonstrations
- Interactive walkthroughs
- A/B testing
- Analytics
- Marketing campaigns

without rebuilding it.

---

## Deliverables BEFORE Coding

1. Product Owner recommendation
2. Demo user journey
3. Information architecture
4. Storyboard of complete experience
5. Standard vs Premium comparison strategy
6. Suggested demo content (messages, contributors, photos, reactions)
7. Site architecture
8. SEO recommendations
9. Analytics events to track
10. Files likely to change
11. Acceptance criteria
12. Risks
13. Future extensibility

Then stop for Founder approval.

Do not implement anything until the specification has been reviewed and approved.

---

## Constraints

- Must use existing MemoryPop design system
- Must be mobile-first
- Must load quickly
- Must work without login
- Must be SEO-friendly
- Must track analytics

---

## User Impact

**Current State:**
- Users click "See a MemoryPop"
- Taken to creation flow
- Don't understand product yet
- High friction, potential drop-off

**Desired State:**
- Users click "Experience a MemoryPop"
- See authentic, emotionally engaging demo
- Understand product deeply
- Feel inspired to create their own
- Understand value of Premium

---

## Success Criteria

Users should finish the demo experience:
1. Understanding what a MemoryPop is
2. Feeling emotionally connected to the concept
3. Wanting to create one for someone they care about
4. Understanding the difference between Standard and Premium

---

## Open Questions

1. What is the ideal demo length? (scroll depth, time on page)
2. Should we use the same demo for all landing pages or occasion-specific demos?
3. How do we balance authenticity with privacy (realistic content without real user data)?
4. What is the conversion funnel from demo → create?
5. How do we measure "understanding" vs just viewing?
