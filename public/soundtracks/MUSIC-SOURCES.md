## Current Problem

The existing soundtrack files (`warm_acoustic.mp3`, etc.) are **sine wave tones generated by ffmpeg**, not real music. This makes the cinematic experience untest able and violates the founder requirement: "For founder beta, the music must actually sound like MUSIC."

---

## Solution: Bensound Royalty-Free Music

[Bensound](https://www.bensound.com) provides high-quality royalty-free music suitable for web projects.

**License:** Free with attribution for non-commercial projects. Commercial license available.

**For Beta:** Using free tier with proper attribution is acceptable. Production requires commercial license (~$5-15 per track one-time).
